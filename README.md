# 🧸 POCKETOY

### Tiny toys, big memories.

Pocketoy is a nostalgic digital toy box that brings childhood favorites back to life as playful browser mini-games.

Inspired by classic pocket toys, handheld gadgets, arcade machines, and Y2K childhood nostalgia, Pocketoy turns each familiar object into an interactive game. Pick a toy, watch it come alive, and press **PLAY**.

---

## 🕹️ Games

### 📱 Pocket Phone
A nostalgic throwback to classic mobile phones and the tiny games that somehow kept us entertained for hours.

### 🎀 Lucky Capsule
Turn the capsule machine and see what surprise is waiting inside.

### 🎣 Magnet Fishing
A digital take on the classic magnetic fishing toy. Catch the little fish and test your timing.

### 💗 Pocket Pet
Your tiny virtual companion. Feed it, play together, put it to sleep, and find it during hide-and-seek.

### 🔴 Pocket Maze
Tilt, navigate, and guide the little ball through a nostalgic handheld maze.

### 🌀 Spin Clash
Choose your spinner, enter the arena, and battle to see whose spinning top survives the clash.

### 💦 Waterloops
Bring back the classic handheld water-ring game. Push, float, and try to land the rings onto the posts.

---

## ✨ The Pocketoy Experience

Pocketoy isn't designed like a traditional game menu.

Instead, the games live inside a whimsical nostalgic toy machine. Each game is represented by a physical childhood toy sitting inside the cabinet.

Hover over a toy and it comes to life:

**Toy → Animation → Game Title → PLAY → Game**

Every toy has its own little interaction, from spinning tops and bouncing capsules to moving water rings, glowing screens, and swinging virtual pets.

The idea is simple:

> **“I remember this toy. I want to touch it.”**

---

## 🚀 Play

Open Pocketoy, choose a toy from the machine, and press **PLAY**.

**Pick a toy. Play a little. Remember a lot.**
